# Site Specification — Women Vibe Coders (`like.html`)

## 📄 Overview
The single-page web app serves as the main portal for **Women Vibe Coders**. It prioritizes extreme clarity, warm community invitation, minimal copy, and direct connection channels.

---

## 🧩 Section Structure

### 1. Header & Hero Section
- **Brand Identity**: `women_vibe_coders_logo_v5.png` icon + "Women Vibe Coders" title.
- **Tagline**: *"A cozy space for women to build intuitively with AI."*
- **Primary CTAs**:
  - `Join Luma Community` (RSVP for upcoming local events)
  - `Hop into Discord` (Connect with members & share wins)

---

### 2. What is Vibe Coding & Recommended Resources
- **Definition Quote**: *"Vibe coding simply means describing a tool in plain language for AI to build."*
- **Intro Header**: *"Here are some recommended resources to learn about vibe coding:"*
- **Interactive Web Preview Cards**:
  1. **LogicTrail Guide**: `https://www.logictrail.app/guides/vibe-coding-for-beginners#what-is-vibe-coding`
     - *Focus*: 4-stage build loop, git snapshots, testing like a stranger.
  2. **DataCamp Beginner Guide**: `https://www.datacamp.com/blog/vibe-coding-guide-for-beginners`
     - *Focus*: Prompting workflows, tool categories, and safety practices.

---

### 3. Bring It To Your City (Host a Local Meetup)
- **Concept**: Empower members to host cozy, low-stress 10-person local gatherings.
- **4-Step Guide**:
  1. **Location**: Pick a quiet café or library room allowing discussion with Wi-Fi & outlets.
  2. **Cozy Luma Event**: Publish a 2-hour event page under our Luma Community capped at ~10 guests so everyone gets heard and attended to.
  3. **Starter Kits**: Download our Welcome Slides (`women_vibe_coders_intro_deck.pptx`) and walkthrough templates.
  4. **Celebrate Wins**: Share event photos/recap on Discord so we can celebrate your chapter's wins on LinkedIn.

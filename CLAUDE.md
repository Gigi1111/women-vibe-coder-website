# CLAUDE.md — Women Vibe Coders Technical & Brand Directives

## 🌟 Brand & Mission
**Women Vibe Coders** is a supportive, non-profit community dedicated to creating an inviting, low-pressure space for women, moms, and first-time builders to build software intuitively with AI.

- **Tone**: Warm, cozy, encouraging, and jargon-free.
- **Logo Mark**: Single-Line Cozy Prompt (`women_vibe_coders_logo_v5.png`) featuring a cozy coffee cup merged with a terminal prompt chevron (`>✦`).
- **Core Channels**: Luma Community (local events & RSVPs) and Discord (peer support & project wins).

---

## 🎨 Design System & Color Palette

- **Background Canvas**: Dark Obsidian (`#07090E`)
- **Primary Accent**: Neon Violet / Lavender (`#A855F7`)
- **Secondary Accent**: Soft Peach / Pink (`#EC4899`)
- **Highlight Accent**: Electric Cyan (`#06B6D4`)
- **Card Fill**: Glassmorphism Slate (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and 1px border `rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.2)`)
- **Typography**: Inter / System Sans, high-contrast, clean 34pt titles and 16-18pt body text.

---

## 🏗️ Architecture & Component Standards

1. **Scrollytelling Motion**: Section snapping with subtle glow transitions.
2. **Interactive Resource Cards**: Clickable web preview cards linking directly to external beginner guides (LogicTrail & DataCamp).
3. **Local Meetup Host Guide**: 4-stage guide for launching cozy 10-guest local chapter meetups.
4. **Live Content Editing (`contenteditable`)**: Toggle between `[Preview | Edit Mode]` to edit site copy directly on screen.
5. **SEO & GEO Optimization**: Embedded JSON-LD schema graphs (`Organization`, `CommunityGroup`, `Event`) for search engine and AI agent discoverability.
